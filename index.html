About Oncare

Local understanding. Regional reach.

Oncare supplies non-pharmaceutical medical products to providers in Kenya and the wider East African region. With experience across healthcare supply, we work with clinicians and medical providers to source equipment, consumables and practical support.

Our aim is to make quality products and responsive service easier to access, with supply planning and logistics built around the needs of the people who care for patients.
